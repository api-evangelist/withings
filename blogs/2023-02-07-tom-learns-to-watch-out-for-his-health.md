Tom Edwards, pictured, is a 29-year-old airline mechanic in Lincolnshire, UK who previously worked as a stone mason and a painter. He says that he enjoys what he does because he meets a lot of people and it’s hands-on work. Indeed he has very talented hands—when not working Tom enjoys photography, adventures in a customized camper van that he started converting during the pandemic, and goldsmithing—more on that later.
