After a heart surgery forced him to rethink his lifestyle, find out how this caregiver learned to change his habits with technology that supported him on his quest for a healthier life. Uwe Papstein is a 61 year old caregiver from Germany who has been working in a nursing home in Denmark for the past 18 years. As a result of his job, he is very familiar with the common health conditions found among elderly people.
