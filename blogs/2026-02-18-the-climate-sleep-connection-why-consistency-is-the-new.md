For years, we’ve defined sleep health by duration, basically, how many hours you clock each night. But a groundbreaking new study¹ shows that sleep regularity—sticking to the same bedtime and wake-up time every day—is a vital, independent predictor of your health. Unfortunately, our changing environment is making it harder and harder to maintain that consistency.
